Reports & GST

Enough to run the shop and to make return filing straightforward. Every register also downloads as CSV.

Stock

  • Current stock list — value at cost and at sale price, with low and out-of-stock items pulled to the top.
  • Only what needs reordering
  • Download the whole stock list as CSV

Trading

  • Sales in a date range — revenue, payment modes and the best-selling items.
  • Purchases in a date range — what the stock cost.
  • Purchase history by dealer — and what each one charged for the same part.

GST

  • Sales register (GSTR-1 help) — invoice-wise, rate-wise, HSN-wise and state-wise, with B2B and B2C separated.
  • Purchase register (input tax credit) — GST paid to dealers, flagging bills that cannot support a claim.

This app does not file returns. It lays the figures out so you or your accountant can key them into the GST portal without adding them up by hand. Always tie the totals back to the portal before filing.

Tally export

  • Masters (import first) — stock items, sales/purchase ledgers, tax ledgers and party ledgers for this range.
  • Vouchers (import second) — sales and purchase entries for this range, ready for Gateway of Tally > Import Data.

Import the masters file first, then the vouchers file — a voucher referencing a ledger that doesn't exist yet will fail to import. Test both files against a spare/test Tally company before importing into your real books, and check the Dr/Cr direction and stock quantity movement look right once imported.

Warranty & history

  • Warranty lookup — by serial number, customer name or phone.
  • Warranties expiring in the next 30 days
  • Already expired
  • Audit log — sign-ins, price changes, cancellations.

Date ranges

Every dated report defaults to this month so far. The financial year runs 1 April to 31 March, which is what the invoice numbering follows.
