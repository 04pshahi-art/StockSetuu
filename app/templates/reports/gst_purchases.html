What to do with these figures

  • The GST here is credit you paid out and can set against the GST you collected on sales. The difference is roughly what you pay the government for the period.
  • CGST and SGST credit can only be set against CGST and SGST. IGST credit is more flexible. Your accountant applies the set-off rules — this screen only lays out the inputs.
  • A bill dated in this period but received later still belongs to the period on the bill. That is why the register goes by bill date, not by when it was keyed in.
  • Reverse charge, imports and capital goods are not modelled here. If any of those apply, tell your accountant separately — this register will not show them.
